Privacy

What happens to your details.

Short version: the check-in collects nothing at all. If you email me or book a call, I use your details only for that, and you can have them deleted any time.

Bluzen is run by Darragh Kearney in the North West of Ireland. I work with people in Ireland and the UK, so I treat both GDPR and UK GDPR as applying. Questions about any of this: info@bluzenfocus.com.

The check-in

Nothing you type or choose in the check-in is saved or sent. It lives in your browser’s memory while you use it and is gone when you close it. There’s no account, no cookies and no tracking, and the page is locked so it can’t send anything over the internet.

If you add it to your home screen, your phone keeps a copy of the check-in’s own files so it works offline. That copy is the page itself, not anything you’ve written.

If you see me one to one, I may give you a client link. Only that link offers to save check-ins, in that browser only, and only when you choose to. You can delete them from the same screen.

This website

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. It doesn’t load fonts, scripts or anything else from other companies. Like any website, the host (GitHub Pages) briefly logs basic technical details, such as an IP address, to keep the site running and secure. I don’t see or use those logs.

If you contact me or book a call

If you email me, I get your email address and whatever you write. If you book a free call, the booking service (Calendly) collects your name and email and passes them to me. I use these only to reply to you or hold the call.

The old quiz

An earlier version of this site had a quiz, a sleep audio sign-up and enquiry forms. They’re gone. Anyone who used them gave their name, email and answers, which were stored with Cloudflare and MailerLite. You can ask me to delete yours at any time.

How long I keep things

Only as long as they’re useful for the reason you gave them. Records connected to client work are kept for the period my insurer and professional body require, and I’ll tell you what that is if you ask.

What you can ask for

A copy of what I hold about you, a correction, or deletion. Email me and I’ll sort it. You don’t need to give a reason.

If you’re not happy

Come to me first if you can. If you’d rather not, or you’re not satisfied with how I handled it, you can complain to the regulator:

  • Ireland: the Data Protection Commission, dataprotection.ie
  • UK and Northern Ireland: the Information Commissioner’s Office, ico.org.uk
