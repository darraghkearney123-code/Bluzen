Privacy

What I do with your details.

Short version. I collect as little as I can, I use it only for the thing you asked for, and you can have it deleted any time by emailing me. Nothing here is sold, and nobody is added to a mailing list for filling in the contact form or a programme enquiry.

Bluzen is run by Darragh Kearney in the north west of Ireland. I work with people in Ireland and the UK, so I treat both GDPR and UK GDPR as applying. If you want anything below explained, just ask me directly at info@bluzenfocus.com.

What I collect, and when

  • The quiz. Your first name, your email, and your answers, including the one you write in your own words. You choose whether to answer that last one.
  • The free sleep audio. Your email address, so I can send it.
  • The Library waitlist. Your email address, so I can tell you when it opens.
  • Programme enquiries. Your name, email and phone number, and for Sensory Kitchens, whether you are asking for an organisation, for someone you care for, or for yourself.
  • The contact form. Your name and whatever you write to me.

Why I hold it

Because you asked me for something, and I need your details to do it. That is the only basis I rely on, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Your quiz answers shape the breakdown I send you and help me understand what people are actually coming with. They are not a medical record, they are not a diagnosis, and no computer decides anything about you on the back of them. I read them myself.

Who else can see it

Three services handle data on my behalf, and only for the jobs below. Some of them process data outside Ireland and the UK.

  • MailerLite, for sending the emails you signed up for.
  • Formspree, which passes form messages to my inbox.
  • Cloudflare, where quiz and programme records are stored.

Beyond that I do not share your details with anyone, and I never sell them. The one exception is the same one that applies to any therapy work: if I believed someone was at serious risk of harm, or I was required by law, I would act on that.

How long I keep it

Only as long as it is useful for the reason you gave it. Marketing details go the moment you unsubscribe or ask. Records connected to client work are held for the period my insurer and professional body require, and I will tell you what that is if you ask.

What you can ask for

A copy of what I hold about you. A correction. Deletion. To withdraw your consent, or to stop the emails, which you can also do from the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any of them.

Email me and I will sort it. You do not need to give a reason, and asking will never affect how I work with you.

If you are not happy

Come to me first if you can. If you would rather not, or you are not satisfied with how I handled it, you can complain to the regulator.

  • Ireland, the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie
  • UK and Northern Ireland, the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk

Bluzen is not a diagnostic or crisis service. If you or someone you care for is in crisis, the numbers on the home page are the right place to go, not this form.
