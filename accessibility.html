Accessibility

Built to be usable by everyone.

The check-in is for anyone who needs it, so it’s built to work with the ways people actually use their phones and computers.

What’s in place

  • Everything works with a keyboard, and with screen readers on phones and computers.
  • Sliders, buttons and the breathing orb all have labels that say what they do.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the moving parts become still pictures.
  • Text and buttons are sized and coloured to be easy to read, and the check-in has a light mode.
  • Nothing times out, and you can go back at any step.

Something not working for you?

If any part of the site or the check-in is hard to use, please tell me and I’ll fix it. Email info@bluzenfocus.com.
