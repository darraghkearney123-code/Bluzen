How it works

Five steps, about three minutes.

  1. What went right. One thing today, however small.
  2. Where your head’s at. A quick 0 to 10, and what’s keeping you there.
  3. Reset. A few rounds of slow breathing. Two breaths in, one long breath out.
  4. Tomorrow. The first sign it’s going a notch better.
  5. One small step. Half a point. Small enough that you’ll actually do it.

Built on solution-focused methods hypnotherapists have used for decades, and on research into breathing, stress and planning. It’s a self-help tool for adults, not therapy or a diagnosis.
