Programmes

Group programme

Sensory Kitchens

A small, hands on cooking group for adults and children with intellectual disabilities. Real cooking, at a pace that suits the room, with the sensory side of a kitchen treated as the point rather than a problem.

The food is the vehicle. Settling, confidence and doing something together are what we are actually after. I work in a specialist training kitchen day to day, and I am a qualified Healthy Food Made Easy facilitator.

Sensory Kitchens runs with a host organisation, so schools, day services and community groups, under their safeguarding and staffing. If you are enquiring for someone you care for, I will let you know when a group is running near you.

One to one, adults

1:1 hypnotherapy

Solution focused hypnotherapy, online, for adults. This is the work I do with people whose sleep has gone, whose head will not switch off, or who have been running on empty for a long time and want somewhere to start.

It works over a course of sessions rather than a single fix, and we talk that through before you commit to anything. Parents and carers are very welcome. This is often the only bit of the week that is about them.

Adults only. Hypnotherapy sits alongside your GP or mental health team, never in place of them, and it is not a crisis service.

About

I'm Darragh Kearney.

I'm a Solution Focused Hypnotherapist based in the north west of Ireland, working online with people across Ireland and the UK.

Alongside my therapy practice, I've spent years working directly with children and adults with additional needs — in schools, day services and respite care — and supporting the parents and carers around them with their own regulation.

That work shapes everything here: plain language, no pressure, and sessions that move at your pace.

Everything here is made with care — every recording, every word.

Credentials

  • Solution Focused Hypnotherapy — CPHT Diploma, 2024
  • Counselling & Psychotherapy — BSc student, PCI College
  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy — Certificate, NWRC 2022
  • Day-to-day experience — Support work and facilitation with neurodivergent children and adults

The honest bit

Bluzen is not a diagnostic service and cannot diagnose any condition. It is not a crisis or emergency service. The quiz, audios and guides are supportive tools, not medical advice, and they don't replace your GP or mental health team.

If you or someone you support is in crisis, please reach out now:

  • Samaritans — 116 123 (Ireland & UK, free, 24/7)
  • Pieta — 1800 247 247 (Ireland)
  • Lifeline — 0808 808 8000 (Northern Ireland)
  • Emergency — 999 or 112
