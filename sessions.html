Sessions

One to one, online, at your pace.

Solution Focused Hypnotherapy for adults, across Ireland and the UK. We work on what you want to be different, not on going back over everything that went wrong.

How it works

  1. A free 15 minute call. We work out whether I can help, and what that would look like. No cost and no pressure.
  2. A block of sessions. Solution-focused work happens over a few weeks, not in one go. Usually six sessions.
  3. Each session. A chat about what’s been good and where you’re at, one small next step, then about twenty minutes of guided relaxation.

Questions people ask

Will I be asleep?

No. Hypnosis feels like being deeply relaxed, a bit like the moment before you drift off. You can hear everything, and you can open your eyes whenever you want.

Am I in control?

The whole time. Nobody can make you do or say anything you don’t want to. No swinging watches, no stage tricks.

Do I have to talk about my past?

No. We focus on what you want now and the small steps towards it. If something from the past comes up, that’s fine, but you don’t have to go there.

How does it work online?

By video call, from somewhere you can sit back and won’t be disturbed. Headphones help for the relaxation part.

Is it confidential?

Yes, within the usual limits: if I believed you or someone else was at serious risk of harm, I would need to act on that. I’ll go through this with you before we start.

What can it help with?

Many people find it helps them feel calmer, sleep better and feel more confident. It suits stress, overthinking and running on empty. It works alongside your GP, never instead of them, and it isn’t a crisis service.
